How we count employees

Active accounts, including freelancers and interns, administrators excluded. A closed account no longer counts, even though it keeps access to its vault. Crossing a threshold? The plan changes the following month, never retroactively.

Frequently asked questions

Is the free plan time-limited?
No. Under ten employees the product is free with no time limit and nothing crippled. We would rather see a small outfit grow with the software than a trial that dies after thirty days.
Can I switch on only part of the product?
Yes. Seven modules — accounting, payroll, e-invoicing, stock and purchasing, treasury, fixed assets, CRM — are off by default and switch on one at a time. While a module is off, its URLs answer 404.
How does migrating my current data work?
A bulk CSV import checks every row before writing, and writes nothing if a single row is invalid. On the Company and Custom plans we prepare the files with you.
Does it replace my accountant?
No. Double-entry accounting, payroll and VAT are there to keep your records and prepare the documents. There is no statutory filing and no electronic submission: the export feeds your accountant.
Do teams need training?
The employee workspace explains itself: leave requests, documents, schedule, directory. The administration workspaces take about half a day to learn, which we cover on the Company and Custom plans.

Start free, change plan when you grow

Under ten employees everything is free. Above that, the pricing fits in three lines. No credit card to try.
