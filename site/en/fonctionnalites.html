01

Foundation and organisation

What holds the rest together: accounts, reporting lines, and the vocabulary shared by every workspace.

  • Accounts and stackable roles: admin, HR, finance, IT, whistleblowing officer
  • Manager status derived from actual reporting lines, never granted by hand
  • Departments and teams, several managers per scope, links re-read on every request
  • Visual org chart, management included, and the people attached to nothing
  • Directory with search and filters, visibility controlled by administrators alone
  • Internal messaging, and work email address configured by the administration
  • Global search partitioned at the source: what you cannot see is not searched
  • Per-person notification centre, with read receipts
  • Bulk CSV import: checked preview, all-or-nothing write
  • Installation wizard, instance settings, seven unlockable modules
  • 16 languages, 3 palettes, light/dark/system theme, responsive interface
Administration console
02

The employee's workspace

The page everyone opens in the morning: what concerns them, and nothing else.

  • Personal dashboard: manager, news, requests, documents awaiting acknowledgement
  • Leave, time-off, absence and remote-work requests: working days computed, weekends excluded
  • Up-to-date leave balance, history of adjustments with their reason
  • Payslips available, and kept fifty years in the digital vault
  • Personal calendar and shared team calendar
  • Weekly schedule, on-call duty, standby shifts
  • Assigned tools with their own login — no third-party password stored
  • Profile: photo, bio, phone, language, password, closing all sessions
  • Stopwatch time tracking for freelancers, one running entry at a time
  • Declaration of interests and gifts, visible to oneself only
Employee workspace
03

Human resources

The employee file, from arrival to departure, with the deadlines that go with it.

  • Request approval with balance deduction, reasoned refusal, cancellation that credits back
  • Payslips: creation, payment tracking, batch generation
  • Company documents with named acknowledgement of receipt
  • Training: catalogue, sessions, enrolment, cost and duration
  • Annual reviews: campaign, template, written record
  • Onboarding and offboarding: tracked checklists, owner and due date per task
  • Skills and certifications, with renewal dates and early warning
  • One-to-ones: shared summary, manager's private notes kept apart
  • Digital vault still reachable after departure, documents sealed by their fingerprint
  • Simple electronic signature: ordered circuit, fingerprint, seal, certificate
  • Fixed-term contracts: the account closes itself on the day
Human resources
04

Recruitment and talent

From applications to day one, with no spreadsheet in between.

  • Job openings and applications tracked stage by stage
  • CV intake: PDF, DOCX or text, written outside the repository
  • Content extraction and a searchable CV library
  • Weighted criteria, score, threshold, ranked applications
  • A hired candidate flows straight into the onboarding checklist
  • Internal surveys and workplace barometer, anonymous by construction
  • Under five answers, no result is shown
Internal requests
05

Health, safety and works council

Obligations that must be written down, and that remind you of themselves.

  • Risk assessment register: work units, hazards, rating, measures, dated review
  • Register of accidents at work and near misses, days lost
  • Protective equipment: named issue, validity period, replacement
  • Medical check-ups: hiring, periodic, return to work, on request
  • Works council: elections, electorate, anonymous ballot, mandates and their expiry
  • Council meetings, benefits and services for employees
  • The management workspace closes itself when the mandate ends
Health and safety at work
06

Finance and administration

Third parties, contracts, the money coming in and the money going out.

  • Customers and suppliers, full record: contacts, contracts, invoices
  • Contracts and above all notice periods: the deadline to terminate is computed and flagged
  • Invoices both ways, gross computed, lateness derived from the due date
  • Budgets by department and financial year, consumption aggregated automatically
  • Expense claims: submission, approval, reimbursement — never one without the other
  • Supplier compliance documents, dated and flagged forty-five days before expiry
  • Scored supplier reviews, the latest one standing rather than the average
  • Multi-currency with the rate frozen when each document is issued
  • Recurring billing: the subscription is the mould, the invoice is the document
  • Automatic reading of incoming invoices and IMAP capture of an accounting mailbox
Finance and administration
07

Accounting, payroll and VAT

Modules, off by default, that switch on when you need them.

  • Double-entry accounting: chart of accounts, journals, trial balance, general ledger
  • An unbalanced entry is refused, and the message says by how much
  • An invoice becomes a journal entry in one click; CSV export for your accountant
  • Payroll engine: configurable rates, gross to net, employer contributions, total cost
  • Detailed payslip line by line, batch generation, simulator
  • VAT returns: output, input, split by rate, credit carried forward
  • A filed return is never recomputed: it is a record, not a dashboard
  • E-invoicing: EN 16931 checks and CII XML export
  • Treasury: accounts, movements, reconciliation, twelve-week forecast
  • Fixed assets: straight-line and declining depreciation, net book value
Double-entry accounting
08

Purchasing, stock and collections

What you order, what you receive, what you are invoiced — and what you are owed.

  • Purchase requests approved by the manager, then by finance above the threshold
  • Purchase orders, goods receipts, and three-way match: ordered, received, invoiced
  • Receiving more than ordered is refused; an invoicing gap is named, not blocked
  • Items, movements, reorder level: stock is the sum of its movements
  • A line linked to an item enters stock the moment it is received
  • Aged customer balance by lateness bracket
  • Staged reminders: first notice, chaser, formal notice, based on what was already sent
  • Every reminder recorded with its level and date
Stock and purchasing
09

Projects, time and profitability

Knowing where a project stands, and what it actually cost.

  • Projects attached to a client, a department, a team and a lead
  • Dated milestones and tasks in columns by status, with priority and estimate
  • Time logged against project and task, at most 24 h per entry
  • Profitability: hours, cost at the hourly rate, remaining margin, share of budget used
  • Three circles of rights: finance, the lead, the members
  • Time per person, and project health on the management dashboard
Projects, milestones and profitability
10

Support and knowledge

Internal and customer requests in one flow, and answers that stay.

  • Internal and customer tickets, priority, origin, assignment
  • First-response target derived from priority, recomputed from the opening time
  • Internal notes invisible to the requester, and not counted as a reply
  • Partitioned by category: an HR request does not leave HR
  • Knowledge base by category, full-text search, adjustable scope
  • Indicators: open tickets, overdue, average delay
Support and tickets
11

Operations and facilities

Scheduling, quality, rooms, vehicles, the front desk.

  • Schedule per person and per day: shifts, on-call, standby, remote work
  • Overlap or approved absence: the slot is refused as it is created
  • Reusable rotations, night shifts, weekly publication
  • Who is on call right now, with their number
  • Quality: non-conformities, root causes, corrective actions and effectiveness checks
  • Internal audits: findings, summary, a finding promoted into a non-conformity
  • Equipment assigned and returned, with the history of holders
  • Rooms and bookings, an overlap refused while naming who holds the slot
  • Fleet: servicing, roadworthiness, insurance, claims, mileage
  • Front desk: visitor register, an on-site list, incoming and outgoing mail
Scheduling, rotations and on-call
12

Management and governance

What management looks at, and what it decides.

  • Dashboard: headcount, absences, invoiced, margin, payroll cost, cash, support load
  • Objectives and key results, each on its own scale, decreasing ones included
  • Meetings: convening, agenda, attendance ticked, minutes
  • Register of decisions with their rationale — what you look for two years later
  • Assigned actions with owner and due date, attached to their decision
  • Risk register: gross and residual rating, 5 × 5 matrix, treatment, review
  • Company events: capacity, automatic waiting list, attendance, budget
  • Deadlines from every workspace consolidated into one sorted list
Management and governance
13

IT and development

The software estate, the accounts, the incidents, and what the team ships.

  • Software estate: seats treated as a constraint, annualised cost, renewals
  • Application access review: closed accounts first, then admin rights
  • IT incidents, timestamped, with mean time to restore actually measured
  • Service catalogue: criticality, owner, repository, documentation
  • Release register per environment and delivery indicators
  • Backups: hourly archive, integrity verified, restore, off-site copy
  • Scoped read-only REST API, signed outgoing webhooks
  • Full instance export as JSON, documents included, secrets excluded
Software estate and access
14

Compliance, corporate and personal data

What a company must be able to prove, kept up as you go.

  • Share register: holdings derived from movements, a transfer written in both legs
  • Corporate mandates with duration, revocation, and expiry flagged in advance
  • General meetings: quorum in shares, resolutions, votes, minutes
  • Delegations of authority: scope, ceiling, duration, expiry flagged
  • Conflicts of interest and gifts: everyone declares for themselves, administration reviews
  • Encrypted internal whistleblowing channel, appointed officers, anonymous follow-up by code
  • Timestamped audit log, exportable, and sealed by fingerprint chaining
  • GDPR: processing register, right of access exported, reasoned erasure
  • Configurable approval flows: forms, thresholds, approvers by role
