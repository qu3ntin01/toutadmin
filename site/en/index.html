Product

Why Toutadmin rather than five tools bolted together

Five pieces of software that don't talk to each other cost more than one, and lose the thing that matters: the overall picture. Toutadmin starts from the opposite idea — one database, workspaces, and rights.

One place, one truth

A department's budget adds up its own invoices and its members' approved expenses. A goods receipt enters stock in the same gesture. Nobody re-keys anything, so nobody contradicts anybody.

Rights are not decoration

Every route carries its own server-side guard, and reporting lines are re-read on every request. A departure cuts access immediately — not when the cookie expires. What you may not see is not searched at all.

Deadlines come to you

Contract notice periods, certifications to renew, medical check-ups, vehicle inspections, corporate mandates, supplier certificates: one engine gathers them all and warns the right person, once, without duplicates.

Every screen says what it does not do

A module shows its limits when you switch it on, not buried in a manual. We prefer an honest sentence to a ticked box: that is what saves you from nasty surprises in month three.

Sixteen languages, properly

Not just the landing screen: statuses, error messages, confirmation dialogs. Arabic mirrors the entire page, because the stylesheet only uses logical properties.

Your data, on your own servers if you want

As SaaS, everything is ready in five minutes. On paid plans you can also host the database on your own server: same software, same updates, data inside your walls.

Security

Security is not a paid extra

Everything below is in the product, whatever the plan — including the free one.

Hardened authentication

Hashed passwords (bcrypt), complexity policy, lockout after repeated failures, TOTP two-factor with recovery codes, sessions revocable one by one or all at once.

Sealed audit log

Every entry carries the fingerprint of the previous one. Changing a line, or removing one from the middle, breaks the chain at that exact point, and the check says which. What it does not do: stop whoever holds the server from rewriting — it makes the rewrite visible.

Internal whistleblowing channel

Mandatory in France from fifty employees. Content encrypted in the database, readable only by the appointed officers — not by administrators, since a report may be about one of them. Anonymous follow-up by code, without an account and without logging.

Frequently asked questions

Is the free plan time-limited?
No. Under five employees the product is free with no time limit and nothing crippled. We would rather see a small outfit grow with the software than a trial that dies after thirty days.
Can I host my data on my own server?
Yes, on paid plans. The software runs on a single machine with Node 22 and SQLite: no database server, no external service. You keep the database, the documents and the backups.
What happens if I leave?
You export the entire instance as JSON, documents included, from the admin console. Nothing is held hostage: it is your database, and it leaves with you in a readable format.
Does it replace my accountant?
No. Double-entry accounting, payroll and VAT are there to keep your records and prepare the documents. There is no statutory filing and no electronic submission: the export feeds your accountant.
Do teams need training?
The employee workspace explains itself: leave requests, documents, schedule, directory. The administration workspaces take about half a day to learn, which we cover on the paid plans.
Can I switch on only part of the product?
Yes. Seven modules — accounting, payroll, e-invoicing, stock and purchasing, treasury, fixed assets, CRM — are off by default and switch on one at a time. While a module is off, its URLs answer 404.

Start free, change plan when you grow

Under five employees everything is free. Above that, €5 per employee per month — tapering from two hundred and fifty — or €10,000 once for the lifetime licence. No credit card to try.
