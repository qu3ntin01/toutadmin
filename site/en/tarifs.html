What support covers, and what it does not

Support covers Toutadmin. It does not cover your hosting: a PHP version to change, folder permissions, a mail server to configure, a firewall closing a port are your host’s business, and they know them better than we do. We tell you what is missing and why — that is what the setup wizard is for, naming every requirement and its remedy. If you would rather not deal with it, assisted setup exists for that.

How licensing works

One key, pasted once at installation. It opens both your licence and your updates, and renews itself every night.

When What happens
While the subscription runsNothing in particular. No banner, no limit.
The subscription has just lapsedEverything keeps working. A banner warns and counts the days you have left — a renewal forgotten on a Friday evening does not stop your company on Monday morning.
Past the grace periodThe instance turns read-only: no more writing, but you sign in, you read everything and you export everything.

What we will never do

Nothing is erased, nothing is encrypted, nothing is hidden. Even in read-only, signing in, the full export, backups and your former employees' vault stay open. You are a data controller under the GDPR: you must be able to leave with your data at any time. An unpaid invoice is settled with a phone call, not by holding payslips hostage.

Without outbound internet access

Some hosting has no route to the outside at all. We then issue an offline licence, which covers the whole subscription and is renewed by file. The trade-off is stated up front: an offline licence cannot be revoked before it expires.

Frequently asked questions

Is the free plan time-limited?
No. Under five employees the product is free with no time limit and nothing crippled. We would rather see a small outfit grow with the software than a trial that dies after thirty days.
What happens if my headcount changes during the year?
The price follows it, from the next invoice. You declare your headcount in your customer area: a hire adds five euros a month per employee, a departure removes them. An invoice already issued never changes, and the first five employees stay free.
Can I switch on only part of the product?
Yes. Seven modules — accounting, payroll, e-invoicing, stock and purchasing, treasury, fixed assets, CRM — are off by default and switch on one at a time. While a module is off, its URLs answer 404.
How does migrating my current data work?
A bulk CSV import checks every row before writing, and writes nothing if a single row is invalid. We can prepare the files with you: that is an option, quoted separately.
Does it replace my accountant?
No. Double-entry accounting, payroll and VAT are there to keep your records and prepare the documents. There is no statutory filing and no electronic submission: the export feeds your accountant.
