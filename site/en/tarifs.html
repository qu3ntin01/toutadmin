How we count employees

Active accounts, including freelancers and interns, administrators excluded. A closed account no longer counts, even though it keeps access to its vault. Crossing a threshold? The change applies the following month, never retroactively — and you come back down the same way.

Frequently asked questions

Is the free plan time-limited?
No. Under five employees the product is free with no time limit and nothing crippled. We would rather see a small outfit grow with the software than a trial that dies after thirty days.
What happens if my headcount changes during the year?
The price follows, both ways, from the following month and never retroactively. A hire that pushes you over a threshold is billed the month after; a departure that brings you back down, likewise. Nobody pays for closed accounts.
Does the lifetime licence include updates?
Yes, with no time limit: that is what the word lifetime means. You get the same versions as the monthly plans, and you can host the database yourself. What the licence does not include is priority support, which stays a separate service.
Can I switch on only part of the product?
Yes. Seven modules — accounting, payroll, e-invoicing, stock and purchasing, treasury, fixed assets, CRM — are off by default and switch on one at a time. While a module is off, its URLs answer 404.
How does migrating my current data work?
A bulk CSV import checks every row before writing, and writes nothing if a single row is invalid. On the paid plans we prepare the files with you.
Does it replace my accountant?
No. Double-entry accounting, payroll and VAT are there to keep your records and prepare the documents. There is no statutory filing and no electronic submission: the export feeds your accountant.

Start free, change plan when you grow

Under five employees everything is free. Above that, €5 per employee per month — tapering from two hundred and fifty — or €10,000 once for the lifetime licence. No credit card to try.
